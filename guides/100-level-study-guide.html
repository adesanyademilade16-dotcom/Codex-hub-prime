100-level study guide for Nigerian university students

Codex Hub is built for 100-level (first-year) university students who need CBT practice, course materials, and clear explanations — not only for entrance exams, but for the semester after admission.

Whether you study at Olabisi Onabanjo University (OOU), University of Lagos (UNILAG), University of Ibadan (UI), Lagos State University (LASU), Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), University of Benin (UNIBEN), University of Nigeria Nsukka (UNN), Federal University of Technology Akure (FUTA), or another Nigerian university, the first year is where habits form: timed practice, weak-topic review, and consistent reading.

What 100-level students usually struggle with

  • New course codes, GNS/GST-style general studies, and heavier reading loads
  • CBT-style continuous assessments that feel different from secondary school tests
  • Balancing lectures, practicals, and personal study without a plan

A simple weekly plan

  • 2–3 short timed drills on Codex Hub CBT
  • One longer mock when you can
  • Review every wrong answer with notes or Nova AI
  • Keep PDFs and course items in your library so revision is one place

After admission vs before admission

If you are still targeting OOU post-UTME / screening, use Codex Prep for OOU-focused practice tools. Once you are on campus as a 100-level student, Codex Hub is for ongoing CBT, Nova, resources, and community.
