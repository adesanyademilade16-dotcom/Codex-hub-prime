Cut-off marks: how to read them (and where to verify)

Every year, students search for cut-off marks for UNILAG, UI, OAU, UNN, LASU, OOU and others. Those numbers are set by each university and change by session.

We do not invent cut-offs. Below is an illustrative summary style based on figures universities publish (example: UNILAG publicly listed merit/catchment-style scores for 2026/2027 programmes). Always verify on the school’s official site and JAMB. Meeting a cut-off does not guarantee admission.

How cut-offs usually work

  • JAMB minimum for a school (e.g. some universities require 200+ UTME and first choice)
  • Departmental / aggregate merit — often higher for Medicine, Law, Engineering, Nursing, Computer Science
  • Catchment scores may differ from merit for some federal universities

Example: competitive programmes (illustrative)

From University of Lagos (UNILAG) public communication for the 2026/2027 merit list style scores (examples only — confirm on unilag.edu.ng):

Other schools (UI, OAU, UNN, LASU, OOU, FUTA, UNIBEN) publish their own lists on different schedules. Use official portals or trusted education publishers only as a pointer — not as a substitute for the university notice.
